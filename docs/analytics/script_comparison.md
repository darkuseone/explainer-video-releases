# Script and hook comparison: evidence boundaries

Read-only repository audit for the 2026-11-07 snapshot. The CSVs have titles but no narration transcripts or exact release-version IDs. A title is not automatically a spoken hook. Archived scripts are evidence of writing, not proof that every word was uploaded or factually correct. No script claim below is endorsed without separate fact-checking.

## Retrieved scripts: exact openings and structural observations

### DNA → dna10 (high topic match; exact upload version unknown)
Source: https://github.com/darkuseone/explainer-video-releases/blob/main/docs/projects/dna10/script.txt

> Ты — программа. И я докажу это за минуту.
> Твой код записан всего четырьмя буквами: А, Т, Г, Ц. Каждая — два бита.

CSV: 499 engaged, 48.59% stayed, 38-second AVD, 66.72% APV, one gained/lost subscriber, 24 likes, three comments. The script leads with an absolute metaphor and promise before naming DNA, contains many figures, and ends by stacking comment/share/subscribe requests and promising simulation 'proof'. Test immediate DNA context, fewer figures and explicit analogy limits. This is a reasoned script critique, not a measured causal diagnosis of its low stop rate.

Illustrative replacement: «ДНК хранит инструкции для клетки, но значит ли это, что человек работает как программа?» A question still needs a concrete visual and a clear payoff; do not make every opening this same template.

### Simulation → sim10
Source: https://github.com/darkuseone/explainer-video-releases/blob/main/docs/projects/sim10/script.txt

> Ты — код. Я это уже доказал. Вопрос: кто его запустил?

CSV: 837 engaged, 63.11% stayed, 37-second AVD, 62.37% APV, two gained subscribers and four comments. The opener presupposes an earlier video. Numbered clues introduce Bostrom, Planck length, light speed and quantum measurement, then a defense. These are not equivalent evidence types. Physical analogies must not be sold as proof or imply space is established to be pixelated. Test a standalone scientific question, fewer concepts and an explicit distinction between philosophical argument, analogy and experiment.

### Oncology → onc10
Source: https://github.com/darkuseone/explainer-video-releases/blob/main/docs/projects/onc10/script.txt

> Вакцина от рака уже есть. Только сделана она для одного человека.

CSV: 781 engaged, 68.99% stayed, 39-second AVD, 65.67% APV; four gained/one lost subscriber. Opening supplies subject and tension, but should clarify personalization rather than implying exactly one beneficiary worldwide. It proceeds through melanoma, pancreatic cancer, CAR-T and mammography, ending with multiple CTAs. Test one coherent mechanism/evidence story; define mRNA/CAR-T immediately or omit them. Do not present clinical trial results as an available cure or use an artificial expert persona to deliver medical advice.

### Garlic → gar10 family
Source: https://github.com/darkuseone/explainer-video-releases/blob/main/docs/projects/gar10/script.txt

> Чеснок против рака — правда или миф? Проверим по исследованиям.

Clear topic and question. Script then moves through preparation, blood pressure, cholesterol, cold, cancer and oil. Proposed writing change: answer the promised cancer-evidence question coherently, distinguish association/trial results and avoid sweeping medical advice. Nine engaged views mean analytics cannot judge this opening or the revised visuals.

### Nuclear Earth-splitting video → nuk10 family
Source: https://github.com/darkuseone/explainer-video-releases/blob/main/docs/projects/nuk10/script.md

> Чтобы расколоть Землю, нужно три целых шесть десятых квинтиллиона Хиросим. У человечества их сто тридцать пять тысяч.

CSV Earth-splitting title: 1,214 engaged, 75.83% stayed, 40-second AVD, 69.36% APV, one gained subscriber. Stronger opening selection coexists with only 0.82 gained subscribers/1k engaged. Script's initial huge numbers and follow-on unit changes create substantial processing demand. Preserve understandable stakes while simplifying scale; never infer that more numbers improve stopping from this one example. This is distinct from the older «Как работает ядерная бомба: три числа» row.

## What to carry forward

Use contextual surprise, not context-free mystique. Preserve logical connections and uncertainty instead of a fragment chain. Let humor clarify a genuine situation. A strong visual or creator approval is not a substitute for audience metrics; audience metrics do not justify misinformation. Every conclusion about why a word works remains a hypothesis until matched transcript/retention evidence and repeated comparable results support it.

# gar10 — Чеснок против рака: правда или миф? — sources

## Facts on screen / in voice
1. Crush, wait ~10 min before heating — Song K., Milner J.A. J Nutr 2001;131(3):1054S–1057S, doi:10.1093/jn/131.3.1054s. Rat DMBA model: 60 s microwave destroyed alliinase activity and the protective effect; 10-min standing after crushing partly preserved it. Animal data.
2. Blood pressure −8.6 mmHg systolic in hypertensives — Ried K. J Nutr 2016;146(2):389S–396S, doi:10.3945/jn.114.202192 (20 RCTs, 970 participants; overall −5.1; hypertensives SBP −8.6, DBP −6.1; no effect in normotensives).
3. Cholesterol ≈ −10% total/LDL if taken >2 months with mildly elevated levels — Ried 2016 review (meta-analysis of 39 RCTs, ~2300 adults). Other meta-analyses mixed.
4. Colds: 1 trial, 146 people (24 vs 65 colds) — Lissiman E. et al. Cochrane 2014, CD006206.pub4: "insufficient clinical trial evidence".
5. Stomach cancer ~half risk (OR 0.54, 95% CI 0.43–0.65), highest vs lowest allium intake — Zhou Y. et al. Gastroenterology 2011;141(1):80–89, doi:10.1053/j.gastro.2011.03.057 (19 case-control + 2 cohort; cohort OR 0.66). Observational, confounding possible.
6. Shandong Intervention Trial — Li W.-Q. et al. BMJ 2019;366:l5016, NCT00339768. 3365 participants (voice rounds to «три тысячи»), garlic extract+oil 7.3 y (1995–2003), follow-up to 2017 (22.3 y). Gastric-cancer mortality HR 0.66 (0.43–1.00) ≈ «на треть ниже»; incidence OR 0.81 (0.57–1.13), not significant (shown on screen).
7. Oil — garlic organosulfur derivatives (diallyl sulfides, ajoene, vinyldithiins) are lipophilic and form in oil macerates: Int J Food Prop 2017, doi:10.1080/10942912.2017.1326055; PMC4237492. No published human trials of garlic + olive or flaxseed oil (only an unpublished Guelph MSc thesis, Arnott 2000). Mediterranean diet benefits not attributable to garlic+oil specifically. Note: garlic-in-oil stored at room temperature = botulism risk (not in video).
8. «Чеснок не заменяет лечение» — voice + on-screen red card.

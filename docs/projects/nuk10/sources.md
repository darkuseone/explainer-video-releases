# nuk10 sources

## Facts
- Gravitational binding energy: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravitational_binding_energy (formula U = 3GM^2/(5R)); Chao 2014, https://doi.org/10.1093/gji/ggu301 (Earth structure context).
- Atmosphere mass: Trenberth & Smith, J. Climate 2005, https://doi.org/10.1175/jcli-3299.1 (5.148e18 kg).
- Hiroshima yield 15 kt: LANL reconstruction https://doi.org/10.2172/1489669 ; ICNND https://www.icnnd.org/reference/reports/ent/part-ii-2.html
- Tsar Bomba 50 Mt: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tsar_Bomba
- World arsenal: FAS Status of World Nuclear Forces https://fas.org/initiative/status-world-nuclear-forces/ ; SIPRI Yearbook 2026 ch. 8 https://www.sipri.org/sites/default/files/YB26%2008%20World%20Nuclear%20Forces.pdf ; total yield ~2 030 Mt from the UN document quoting FAS 2023 https://documents.un.org/doc/undoc/gen/n23/324/29/pdf/n2332429.pdf (includes retired warheads; upper-bound estimate)
- Chicxulub energy ~1e23 J: https://www.nature.com/articles/s43017-022-00283-y ; https://doi.org/10.1063/pt.htyz.zmzd ; arXiv:1403.6391
- Solar luminosity 3.828e26 W and the astronomical unit (IAU 2015 B3 / 2012 B2).
- Nuclear winter, >5 billion at risk of famine: Xia et al. 2022, Nature Food https://www.nature.com/articles/s43016-022-00573-0
- Kopp & Lean 2011 (solar constant): https://doi.org/10.1029/2010GL045777 ; Cannikin test (largest US underground test, 5 Mt) https://doi.org/10.2172/4701483 (context only)
- Stress checks: gramota.ru (килотонна, квинтиллион, квадриллион, триллион), ru.wiktionary / ru.wikipedia (гигатонна, Чиксулуб).
